# Human Handoff Brief Contract

## Executive evidence summary

Up to ten bullets. Each bullet must contain:

- finding;
- why it matters;
- artifact reference;
- caveat.

## Model comparison

A compact table containing:

- candidate;
- feature view;
- validation design;
- RMSLE;
- stability;
- complexity;
- leakage risk;
- selected status.

## Main findings

Three to five findings.

For each:

### Finding title

- Evidence:
- Magnitude:
- Interpretation:
- Limitation:
- Possible presentation role:

## Submission status

- selected model:
- row count:
- schema:
- non-negative check:
- finite-value check:
- identity/order strategy:
- output path:

## Risks

Group into:

- data integrity;
- validation;
- model;
- interpretation;
- delivery.

## Limitations

State what the analysis cannot establish.

## Remaining uncertainties

Only include uncertainties that require human judgment or could change the presentation.

## Suggested narrative

Provide a sequence of claims and evidence, not a final recommendation.

## Questions for the human consultant

Questions must require judgment, prioritization, or communication choices.
